The My Clippings.txt file from your Kindle.

Where is it?

Connect the Kindle by USB and open its documents folder: /Volumes/Kindle/documents/ on macOS, the Kindle drive on Windows, /media/<user>/Kindle/documents/ on Linux.

Apple Books

Two database files from a Mac: annotations and books.

Where are they?

In Finder press Cmd+Shift+G and open ~/Library/Containers/com.apple.iBooksX/Data/Documents/. The annotations file is in AEAnnotation/, the books file in BKLibrary/; pick the .sqlite file in each.
